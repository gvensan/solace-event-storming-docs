Everything you need to open the tool, learn the method, and print the companion one-pagers. Facilitators: fill in the app address below before you send this out.

The application

WHERE App address:

Your facilitator shares the link - or the invite link, which opens the right board directly. Write it in before printing or mailing this card.

FACILITATORS Register your own account

On the facilitator screen, pick Register: any username (3+ characters) and password (8+ characters). No shared credentials.

PARTICIPANTS No account - just the invite link

Join with a display name. The link itself sets your role: participant (edit, vote, chat) or observer (read-only, watch and read).

BROWSER Desktop or laptop

A window at least 1024px wide. The board is not built for phones or tablets - chat and reading work, the wall does not.

First five minutes

  1. Open the app address and register, then sign in.
  2. Open the Samples tab and click Solly's Coffee - you get your own complete copy of a finished workshop to explore safely.
  3. You land on the big-picture map. The pills under the toolbar are the use-case boards - click one to drill in.
  4. Ready for real work? New workshop starts a blank one - every workshop opens on its map.

Good to know

The wall is event-sourced: every change is kept, undo is safe, and the whole session can be replayed.

Views are personal lenses: plain, actor, system, context - switching yours moves nothing for anyone else.

Deliverables live in the export menu: the workbook, board pictures, and the Event Portal import file.
