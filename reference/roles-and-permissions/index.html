Roles and permissions

Every board has three roles. This page is the full capability matrix; the short version is that the facilitator drives, participants contribute, and observers watch.

  • Facilitator runs the session: phases, timers, moderation, enrichment, lifecycle, and exports. Almost everything that is gated is gated to the facilitator.
  • Participant contributes to the wall: stickies, votes, links, context proposals, and chat.
  • Observer watches: they see the board and roster and can take part in the chat, but cannot edit the board, vote, present, invite, or export.

Voting on a map is only ever on Domain cards, and it decides sequence - which slice the room storms next. There are no phases, no flow links and no per-board Event Portal export on a map; see Running the Big Picture map.

How you get a role

Section titled “How you get a role”
  • Facilitators sign in with an account and open or create a board from the home screen.
  • Participants and observers join through a role-scoped invite link. The facilitator shares two links, one for participants and one for observers, and the link a person opens determines their role. They can join with an account (stable identity across sessions) or as a name-only guest.

The role is server-enforced: it rides in a signed invite the server verifies, so an observer link cannot be edited into a participant one, and a client can never claim a role it was not granted. From there the server assigns each member’s role and enforces the role boundaries - observers cannot change the board (no stickies, votes, links, or proposals), and facilitator-only actions (enrichment, plan tags, lifecycle, moderation, phases) are rejected server-side. Chat is the one thing observers can write. A few finer limits, such as only deleting your own sticky, are enforced in the app.

Footnotes

Section titled “Footnotes”

  1. On a moderated board, a participant’s new stickies go to the facilitator’s submission tray for approval instead of landing on the wall directly. When the board is not moderated, they appear immediately. ↩ ↩2

  2. One vote per person per sticky (retractable). Voting is the single hard phase gate: it is rejected during the two alignment phases and opens at storming. Totals and voter names are public. ↩

  3. Participants cannot delete links, but they can undo their own draws. ↩

  4. Anyone may propose a boundary; the room votes and the facilitator approves or rejects it (even their own). Boundary creation is always moderated. ↩

  5. Everyone adds terms; the proposer or facilitator edits; the facilitator deletes. ↩

  6. Enrichment and plan tags are a facilitation act: participants dictate, the facilitator types. The side panel is visible to everyone. ↩ ↩2

  7. These are lenses - they change only what you see, never the board; that is why observers have them too. ↩
