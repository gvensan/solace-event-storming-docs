Shortcuts and gestures

Everything on the board is reachable with a handful of gestures. This page is the consolidated reference; the participant guide explains them in context.

Mouse and trackpad

Section titled “Mouse and trackpad”
GestureWhat it does
Drag the wallPan
Scroll wheelZoom in and out (in steps)
Trackpad pinchZoom smoothly
Double-click the wallCreate a sticky of the selected kind at that spot (plain view and the ◷ origin lens)
Double-click a stickyEdit its text
Drag a stickyMove it (plain view); in lane views, left-right reorders on the timeline and dropping onto another lane reassigns it
Drag a sticky into a drawn boundaryAssign it to that context (dropping outside clears it)
Click a sticky, then Alt-click (⌥-click) anotherDraw a flow link between them
⤳ button on a selected sticky, then click a targetDraw a flow link (the discoverable sibling of Alt-click)
Hover a sticky, click the vote controlCast or retract your vote
Click a wireSelect the link (facilitators can then delete it)
Click a kind in the paletteSelect it for creation and spotlight all stickies of that kind
Click the minimapJump the camera to that part of the wall

Keyboard

Section titled “Keyboard”
KeyWhat it does
EnterCommit the sticky text you are editing
Shift+EnterNew line inside the sticky text
EscCancel an edit, clear the kind spotlight, cancel an armed link draw, close a dialog
Delete / BackspaceDelete the selected sticky (your own; facilitators can delete any) or the selected link (facilitator, with confirmation)
fZoom to fit the whole board

The zoom controls

Section titled “The zoom controls”

The corner widget carries the same navigation as the gestures: zoom in, zoom out, fit to content, and reset to 100%. The camera also fits to content automatically when you first enter a board and when you switch views.

Where gestures work

Section titled “Where gestures work”

Views deliberately differ in what they allow:

RawPlainLane views
Create✓✓-
Edit, vote, delete✓✓✓
Move-✓reorder / reassign
Draw links-✓✓
See links-✓✓

Raw is the wall as it was born: you can add to the storm and read it, but its positions are history, not layout. See Working on the board.
