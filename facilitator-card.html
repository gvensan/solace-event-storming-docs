Before the phases: the map

Every workshop opens on its Big Picture map, and the five phases below belong to the boards you spin out of it. On the map the room floods coarse events (past tense, business-wide), parks hotspots, names Domain cards (first line the name, the rest a one-line pitch) and Team cards, then uses the domains and teams views to seat who is expected to own what. Dot votes on the map go on domain cards, and they decide sequence, not merit. Approving a domain card creates its board - empty, named from the card, in the rail for everyone. No phases, no timers, no links on a map: a seat is how you say who owns something.

Phases, timeboxes, and the exit cue for each

PhaseDefaultMove on when...
1 · Concept30 minthe cast is on the wall and the key terms are defined - the room stops renaming things
2 · Use case20 minyou can say the chosen journeys out loud and nobody adds a fourth
3 · Storming75 minthe timeline reads left to right and new stickies are rephrasings of old ones
4 · Architecture50 minevery important event carries enough for architects to continue (topic, QoS, owner), every policy names the system that runs it, and back on the map no ownership conflict is left open
5 · Build plan30 minno hotspot without an owner or a decision, no "now" without a name

Defaults fit a 4-hour workshop; tune in ⚙ BOARD. Phases never auto-advance, and the phase switch briefs everyone - you included. Pause explicitly at phase 4: "we discovered how the business works; now we design how a system tells it."

Standing moves

Convert every argument into a hotspot or a vote - never chase it mid-flow.

Topic names or QoS opinions surfacing mid-storm? Park as hotspots - architecture has its own phase.

Read the wall: dense clusters are the room's energy; gaps are what nobody wants to talk about. Walk toward the gaps.

Enrichment is single-driver: the room dictates, you type. Pivotal-and-voted events first.

Ask of every policy: "whose code runs this rule?" - that system tag is the ONLY thing that makes it a subscriber. No system, no subscribe relation in the export.

Boundary proposals are always moderated - including your own. Collect a reason when rejecting.

Voting opens at storming, never before - early votes poison the signal.

Use ✨ auto-layout when chaos stops being productive; use presenting when the whole room must look at one thing - it locks their cameras, so ask first.

Watch the members roster (👥): it counts who is actually here, not who was invited.

Acts that deserve the pause the confirm gives you

Kick-all ends the session for everyone and closes the room to joins.

Clear wall / delete board land on every screen at once; deletion cascades and is permanent.

Finalize locks the storm - enrichment, plan tags and exports continue; reopen is one click, but announce it.

Exit kit: workbook + board file + EP import file + diagram (see the handoff sheet). The board file alone can resurrect the whole session, history intact.
