Cheatsheets

Four printable one-pagers. Each is a standalone poster that prints to a single A4 page and carries a Save as PDF button, so a facilitator can mail one out before a session or hand one across a table after it. They open in a new tab.

Before you walk in

For participants, before the session. The one rule (events are past tense), the sticky vocabulary as actual stickies, how they connect, the rules of the room, and what the session’s arc will feel like. Five minutes, no preparation possible.

Open the participant primer ↗

Facilitator field card

For facilitators, during the session. Per-phase timeboxes and exit cues, the storm’s four waves, the standing moves that keep a room productive, and the acts that deserve the pause their confirm gives you.

Open the field card ↗

From the wall to Event Portal

For architects, at the handoff. What every wall element becomes in the Event Portal import file, what deliberately stays workshop-side, and why publishing needs one tag while subscribing needs two.

Open the translation map ↗

After the workshop

For the team that carries the model forward. The four artifacts a finished session produces, what each one is for, and the first three things to do with them.

Open the handoff kit ↗
