After the workshop: your handoff kit

for the team that carries the model forward

A finished session produces four artifacts. Together they are the whole workshop - the model, the decisions, and the ability to pick the session back up. This sheet says what each one is for and what to do first.

The four artifacts

.XLSX The workbook

Everything as a spreadsheet: events with topics/QoS/owners, contexts, the build plan, the glossary, and Hotspots & Decisions - the questions the room parked, resolved or still open. The sheet most people will actually read.

.JSON The Event Portal import file

Domains, applications, events, schemas, topic addresses and pub/sub relations - imported through EP's own UI, or pushed into a tenant from the toolbar, everything arriving as DRAFT for your governance to review. One board exports one domain; from the map, the merged workshop file covers every board with one event per name. The translation dictionary is the companion "wall to Event Portal" sheet.

.SVG The architecture diagram

The applications-and-events graph as a picture - arrows into an event mean "publishes", arrows into an application mean "consumes". For slide decks and corridor conversations.

.BOARD.JSON The board file

The session itself - every sticky, link, vote and decision with full history. Import it into any instance of the tool to resume, extend, or replay the workshop exactly as it happened.

First three things to do

  1. Import the .json into Event Portal (Designer → import), then walk the DRAFT objects against the translation sheet. Promote what survives review; the workshop proposes, your governance disposes.
  2. Work the decision backlog. Open the workbook's Hotspots & Decisions sheet: every open hotspot has an owner - hold them to it. Resolved ones carry the room's recorded decision; treat those as agreements, not suggestions.
  3. Keep the board file somewhere safe. It is the only artifact that can resurrect the session - schedule the follow-up storm from it rather than starting a fresh wall.

Fine print worth knowing

"Schema TBD" on an event means the room deliberately skipped the payload - a follow-up, not an error.

QoS and owning team arrive inside event descriptions - EP has no first-class fields for them yet.

Nothing was inferred from layout. Every pub/sub relation traces to a link or attribute the room explicitly accepted.

Questions about any element's origin? The board file replays the entire session - every object in the export can be traced to the moment the room created it.
